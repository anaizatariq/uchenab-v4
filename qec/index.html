Overview

Institutional Performance Evaluation

‘The Quality Enhancement Cell (QEC)’ has been a part of University of Chenab since the very beginning. According to the quality assurance groundwork of Higher Education Commission it is essential for any educational institute to have a quality enhancement cell so as to maintain a proper check and balance on both academic as well as research field.

Introduction

Quality Education has always been the prime focus of the University of Chenab. The Management ensures quality in every dimension and manner whether it is to provide infrastructure and ancillary facilities to students or to develop intellect in them.

Mission

The University of Chenab resolves to guarantee its stakeholders that the quality of the university education is maintained and enhanced as per HEC guidelines and international standards, also procedures and protocols are regularly checked for transparency and objectivity to achieve quality enhancement.

Primary Functions

As per the instructions of HEC, the primary responsibilities of the QEC of University of Chenab are as follows:

Public confidence

  • Promote public confidence that quality and standards are enhanced and safeguarded.
  • Review quality standards and teaching/learning in each subject area.
  • Review academic affiliations for effective management of programs.

Explicit standards

  • Define clear, explicit standards as points of reference for reviews.
  • Apply Pakistan Qualification Framework for curriculum development.
  • Develop standard program specifications for knowledge, skills, and attributes.

Quality assurance

  • Develop processes and evaluation methods to affirm quality provision.
  • Foster curriculum, subject and staff development, and research.
  • Ensure alignment with national arrangements for Higher Education per HEC.

QEC Procedures

QEC at the University of Chenab is also responsible to develop procedures for the following:

Annual monitoring and evaluation including program, faculty, and student perceptions
Departmental Review
Self-Assessment Reports
QA through Post Graduate Program Review of MS, MPhil, and PhD degrees
Institutional assessment
Continuous Quality Improvement (CQI)
Implementation of all HEC Surveys

Activity Calendar

The Quality Enhancement Cell publishes an annual activity calendar that schedules every quality assurance activity across the academic year—program monitoring, self-assessment cycles, surveys, reviews, and reporting to HEC.

Program Monitoring

Semester-wise monitoring of teaching, faculty and student feedback across all departments.

Self-Assessment Cycle

Initiation of SARs, assessment team visits, exit meetings and implementation plans.

Postgraduate Review

Review activities for MS, MPhil and PhD programs per HEC criteria.

HEC Surveys

Administration and reporting of all HEC-mandated surveys.

Accreditation

Coordination with the respective accreditation councils for undergraduate programs.

Reporting

Submission of executive summaries and annual quality reports to the University authorities and HEC.

For the detailed dated calendar of activities, please contact the QEC office at ad.qec@uchenab.edu.pk.

Self PostGraduate Program Review

The quality of MS, MPhil, and PhD level programs are peer reviewed keeping in view the minimum criteria of HEC to enhance their quality to make them internationally compatible.

The Review Procedure

The QEC at UChenab conducted the Post Graduate Program Review (PGPR) on March 2024, as per the HEC guidelines. The procedure is as follows:

1
Program Manuals

Program manuals were prepared on prescribed HEC pro-formas by the respective departments.

2
Panel Review

Program Manuals were reviewed by a panel comprising internal as well as external members:

  • Dr. Ahmad Adeel (Convener) - Chairperson of Business Education
  • Mr. Furqan Durrani (Internal) - Assistant Director QEC
  • Mr. Syed Faizan Haider (External) - Director QEC UoL
  • Mr. Hafeez Rafiq (External) - Manager QEC UoL
3
Detailed Report

A detailed report was prepared by the Review Panel and shared with the worthy Pro Rector. The quality of all MS/MPhil programs was reported to be Satisfactory.

Self-Assessment Process

HEC requires universities to conduct periodic self-assessments for their academic programs to improve them and ensure high academic standards. Self-assessment is an important tool for educational quality assurance and provides feedback for faculty and administration to initiate action plans for improvement.

To achieve the objectives as mentioned above, Self-Assessment Reports (SAR) about each program were initiated in the light of the HEC Self-Assessment Manual.

The Procedure (2023-24)

1

QEC Initiated

The QEC initiated the SA one semester before the end of the assessment cycle through the Pro-Rector Office. Upon receiving the initiation letter, the department formed a program team (PT) responsible for preparing a SAR. The department submits the SAR to QEC through the Chairperson. QEC reviews it within one month.

2

Pro-Rector

The Pro-Rector formed a program assessment team (AT) in consultation with QEC, comprising senior faculty and the Chairperson. QEC schedules the AT visit. The AT conducts the assessment and presents findings in an exit meeting. QEC submits an executive summary to the Pro-Rector.

3

Department

The Department prepares and submits an implementation plan to QEC based on AT findings, including corrective actions, responsibility, and timeframe. QEC follows up on the plan to ensure adherence, reviewing progress once a semester.

Accreditation & Memberships

Objective of this section is to assure that all the undergrad level programs of HEIs are accredited by its respective accreditation council. For the purpose, the section administers the activities in respect of establishing new accreditation councils and coordinating with existing councils, assessing their performance and build their capacity.

University Administration has a strong focus and resolve to bring all administrative, management, and academic activities into the ambit of quality assurance. To include his opinion in the formation and implementation of various policies, QEC has been included in numerous authorities.
