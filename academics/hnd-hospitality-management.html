Program Overview

(HND) Hospitality Management

The Higher National Diploma (HND) in Hospitality Management at the University of Chenab is designed to equip students with the skills and knowledge necessary for a successful career in the hospitality industry. This program provides a comprehensive understanding of hospitality operations, management practices, and customer service excellence.

  • Internationally Recognized Qualification: Offered by Pearson Edexcel, London, UK.
  • Global Reach: Pearson Edexcel operates in 112 countries, with over four million students annually pursuing its internationally recognized qualifications.
  • Equivalent to Two-Year BA/BSc in Pakistan: Providing a solid foundation for further academic pursuits.
  • Affordable and Economical: Accessible education without compromising on quality.
  • Direct Employment Opportunities: In-depth professional education highly regarded by employers.
  • Pathway to Further Education: Successful completion of HND allows direct entry to a one-year top-up to a BA/BSc Honors Degree at UK universities.
  • Early Professional Training: Ideal for students looking to start their professional journey early.
  • Assignment-Based Assessment: UCI supports students in completing their HND on time.

Modules

Year 1: Core Foundations

  1. Introduction to Hospitality Management

    • Overview of the hospitality industry, key concepts, and career opportunities.

  2. Customer Service and Experience Management

    • Techniques for delivering exceptional customer service and creating positive guest experiences.

  3. Food and Beverage Operations

    • Management of food and beverage services, including menu planning, cost control, and service standards.

  4. Front Office Operations

    • Essentials of front office management, including guest check-in/check-out procedures, reservations, and handling guest inquiries.

  5. Hospitality Marketing

    • Strategies for marketing hospitality services, including digital marketing, market research, and brand management.

  6. Financial Management in Hospitality

    • Fundamentals of financial management, budgeting, and financial planning specific to the hospitality industry.

Year 2: Advanced Topics and Specialization

  1. Event Management

    • Planning and managing events such as conferences, weddings, and corporate functions.

  2. Hospitality Law and Ethics

    • Legal and ethical issues in hospitality management, including health and safety regulations and employment law.

  3. Human Resource Management in Hospitality

    • Recruitment, training, and development of staff, along with managing employee relations in a hospitality context.

  4. Strategic Management in Hospitality

    • Strategic planning and decision-making processes to drive business growth and operational efficiency.

  5. Sustainability and Green Practices in Hospitality

    • Implementing sustainable practices and environmental management in hospitality operations.

  6. Hospitality Industry Trends and Innovations

    • Analysis of current trends, emerging technologies, and innovations shaping the hospitality industry.

  7. Work Placement and Practical Experience

    • Real-world experience through placements in various hospitality settings, applying theoretical knowledge in practical scenarios.

  8. Project Management in Hospitality

    • Managing hospitality projects from conception to completion, including planning, execution, and evaluation.

Assessment

All the modules are assessed by coursework, including individual assignments, real-business case studies, interview projects, online quizzes, group work, and poster presentations.

Duration

The minimum duration for completion of the HND – Art & Design is two years. 

Entry Requirement

At least 2 ‘A’ levels and 5 subjects of ‘O’ levels, Intermediate, Matric and Work-related experience. Appropriate English language skills.
