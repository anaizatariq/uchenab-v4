Associate Degree in Accounting and Finance

Apply Now
Popular: Computing | Business | Health
    Duration 2 Years (4 Semesters)
    Credit Hours 66 Credits
    Intake Spring & Fall

    Program Overview

    Meticulously designed to provide a strong foundational knowledge of accounting and finance principles, integrating practical and theoretical aspects of business management.

    Curriculum

    The degree structure below outlines the typical sequence of courses across four semesters.

    • Functional English 3 CR
    • Introduction to Sociology 2 CR
    • Introduction to Business 3 CR
    • Introduction to History 2 CR
    • Introduction to Computer Application 3 CR
    • Ideology and Constitution 2 CR

    • Expository Writing 3 CR
    • Introduction to Financial Accounting 3 CR
    • Islamic Studies 2 CR
    • Environmental Sciences 3 CR
    • Principles of Management 3 CR
    • Entrepreneurship 2 CR
    • Civics & Community Engagement 2 CR

    • Principles of Micro Economics 3 CR
    • Quantitative Reasoning-1 3 CR
    • Financial and Regulatory Institutions 3 CR
    • Principles of Marketing 3 CR
    • Financial Accounting 1 3 CR
    • Fundamentals of Business Finance 3 CR

    • Quantitative Reasoning-ll 3 CR
    • Managerial Finance 3 CR
    • Introduction to HRM 3 CR
    • Financial Accounting ii 3 CR
    • Principles of Macro Economics 3 CR

    Career Outcomes

    Accountants, Financial Analysts, Auditors, Tax Consultants, Financial Managers, and transition into BS Accounting & Finance.
