Physical Therapy (MS)

This degree program is tailored for graduates seeking to specialize in physiotherapy for patients with certain disorders.

Program Overview

This degree program is tailored for graduates seeking to specialize in physiotherapy for patients with certain disorders. It focuses on equipping students with the necessary professional skills to work effectively as part of rehabilitation teams within the disabled community. Through practical training and hands-on experience, graduates will develop the expertise needed to provide specialized physiotherapy services tailored to the unique needs of individuals with different conditions.
Moreover, the program places a strong emphasis on research and education-based practices in physiotherapy. Students will engage in rigorous coursework and research initiatives aimed at advancing the field and improving patient outcomes. By combining practical skills with a strong foundation in evidence-based practice, graduates will be well-prepared to make significant contributions to the healthcare sector as skilled and compassionate physiotherapy professionals.

Modules

The Master of Science in Physical Therapy is a comprehensive two-year program designed for students seeking advanced knowledge and skills in physiotherapy.
Our program offers a blend of core and elective courses. Core courses cover fundamental topics in physiotherapy, including musculoskeletal assessment, therapeutic interventions, and rehabilitation techniques. Elective courses span a wide range of specialized areas such as sports physiotherapy, pediatric rehabilitation, neurorehabilitation, and pain management.
The first year focuses on core modules essential for building a strong foundation in physiotherapy practice. In the second year, students have the flexibility to choose elective modules based on their interests and career goals.
During the second year, students engage in research activities, exploring modern advancements in physiotherapy and developing solutions for current clinical challenges. This research component allows students to contribute to the evolving field of physiotherapy and enhance their analytical and problem-solving skills.
By the end of the program, graduates are equipped with advanced knowledge, practical skills, and research experience, positioning them for successful careers as skilled and innovative physiotherapy professionals.

Eligibility Criteria

16 years of education, 5 years Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) or 4 yeras BS Phhsical Therapy from HEC recognized Institute with minimum 2.5/4.00 CGPA, University Entry Test (60% marks) or GAT/NTS-General Test- with minimum 50% marks, interview is mandatory .

Tuition Fee

Tuition fees cover the majority of the costs of your study, including teaching and assessment. Fees are charged at the start of each academic semester. If you pay your fees directly to the University, you can also choose to pay in instalments.

Additional Course Cost
There may be extra costs related to your course for things such as stationery, books, materials, and field trips.

Teaching

The teaching approach in the MS in Physical Therapy program is objective-oriented and practical-based, emphasizing research-based knowledge delivery by highly qualified instructors. Our state-of-the-art lecture rooms and labs are equipped with modern learning tools. We employ various teaching methodologies such as assignments, presentations, quizzes, seminars, group discussions, and activities to enhance learning experiences.
Key aspects of our program include:
– Each subject is covered through 30 to 32 lectures, with 2-4 hours of teaching per week based on credit hours.
– Classes are coordinated by faculty members.
– Training sessions are evaluated by consulting body consultants and graded accordingly.
– Students are required to maintain at least 75% attendance in each course per semester.
– The Quality Assurance department monitors program components according to the study scheme to ensure high standards of education.

Assessment

All the modules are assessed by coursework, including individual assignments, case study analysis, projects, online quizzes, group work, and presentations.

Careers
