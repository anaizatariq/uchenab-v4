Faculty Overview

The Faculty of Physical and Biological Sciences at The University of Chenab aims to provide a solid foundation of knowledge. They work in the fields of physics as well as mathematics. Our faculty is all about highly professional educators who are loyal to promote academic excellence and intellectual growth.

We offer a range of undergraduate and graduate degree programs in physics and mathematics. They are all designed to equip students with the theoretical and practical knowledge to succeed in their chosen careers. Our programs are available to meet the needs of both aspiring professionals. It hence helps those seeking to pursue further academic research.

Main Mission

In the near future, we are planning to include degrees in anatomy, zoology biology, biochemistry, biotechnology and biological sciences. Our faculty is loyal in staying up-to-date with the latest developments in these fields. They are hence incorporating new teaching methodologies to enhance the learning experience for our students.

Our state-of-the-art facilities and cutting-edge research labs enable students to engage in hands-on learning. We hence conduct research that contributes to advancements in the fields of physics and mathematics. Meanwhile, we encourage our students to participate in research projects and collaborate with faculty members to deepen their understanding of these subjects.

At the Faculty of Physical and Biological Sciences, we are committed to providing an innovative and supportive learning environment that inspires students to pursue their passions and achieve their goals.
