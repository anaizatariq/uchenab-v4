Program Overview

The Department of Business Education at the University of Chenab offers a Bachelor of Studies in Accounting and Finance (BSAF) program. It is available for students interested in the rapidly growing area of business. The program is a four-year full-time undergraduate course. It provides students with professional skills and knowledge. Students can employ in contemporary national and international businesses, financial institutions, as well as various organizations. The program combines the study of Accounting with Finance to develop a broader business perspective. It gives an in-depth knowledge of fundamental concepts of financial management, corporate accounting, investment analysis, capital budgeting, as well as economic processing in global financial markets.

Program awareness

The program emphasizes critical awareness of accounting and finance practices. Meanwhile, it is providing students with skills to maintain a financial report on the performance of an organization. Well, it hence describes the financial structure and viability of an organization. Through Finance, learn to evaluate the role of an organization in raising money and capital. You can also learn how to allocate resources among competing investment as well as savings opportunities.

Advantages

The program also enhances students’ leadership skills, communication, as well as teamwork through real-life case studies and work experience. Students have the option to focus on accounting, finance, or a mixture of both through a wide range of courses. An optional placement year allows students to gain work experience with accountancy firms, investment banks, and other renowned national as well as international employers. Alternatively, students can hence choose coursework instead of a placement year. Overall, the BSAF program aims to cultivate skilled and proficient individuals in the fields of accounting and finance.

Modules

Our Bachelor of Science in Accounting and Finance is a comprehensive four-year program designed to equip students with the essential skills and knowledge needed for a successful career in accounting and finance. Throughout the program, students will hence gain a deep understanding of key concepts in accounting, finance, and business management. It will be also helpful in developing critical analytical as well as problem-solving skills.

Year One: Foundations

The first year lays the groundwork for advanced study in accounting as well as finance. Students will be introduced to:

  • Financial Accounting:Understanding the principles and standards of financial reporting.
  • Management Accounting:Learning how to use accounting information for internal decision-making.
  • Financial Management: Exploring the fundamentals of financial planning, investment, as well as control.
  • Business Law:Gaining insights into the legal environment affecting businesses.
  • Statistics:Developing the ability to analyze as well as interpret data.
  • Business Analytics:Learning to leverage data for strategic business decisions.

Year Two: Specialization and Planning

In the second year, students can hence tailor their studies by choosing to focus on accounting, finance, or a combination of both. Available modules include:

  • Global Business Environment:Understanding the impact of globalization on business practices.
  • Corporate Finance:Delving into financial strategies as well as corporate governance.
  • Financial Reporting:Mastering the preparation and analysis of financial statements.
  • Financial Marketing: Exploring marketing principles within the financial sector.
  • Entrepreneurial Mindset:Developing skills for innovation as well as business creation.

Year Three: Real-World Experience

The third year hence features a work placement module, providing students with the opportunity to apply their skills in a professional setting. This hands-on experience is hence complemented by advanced modules such as:

  • Critical Issues in Management:Analyzing contemporary management challenges.
  • Business Taxation:Understanding tax principles as well as their impact on businesses.
  • Auditing and Accountability: Learning the principles and practices of auditing.
  • Investment Management:Exploring portfolio management as well as investment strategies.
  • Fintech:Examining the intersection of finance and technology.
  • Entrepreneurial Finance and Venture Capital:Understanding financing options for startups.
  • Derivatives and Risk Management:Studying financial instruments as well as risk mitigation strategies.

Eligibility Criteria

1.Graduates with a minimum CGPA of 2.0 out of 4.0 or at least 45% marks in a 2-year associate degree in business studies (semester system) are eligible to apply for direct admission into the 5th semester of the BBA and BS A&Fs, in accordance with HEC guidelines and the university’s admission policy. 2. All other students, including those with an ADP in business studies (annual system), ADP in non-business fields, or other equivalent 14-year degrees (such as B.A./B.Sc.), are required to complete 16 credit hours of deficiency courses in a bridge semester (UGE-2023; National Education Framework-2015), as approved by the departmental curriculum review committee (details provided below).
3. After completing the bridge semester with a minimum CGPA of 2.0, students will join the regular fifth semester of their degree program.

Tuition Fee

Tuition fees cover the majority of the costs of your study, including teaching as well as assessment. Fees are hence charged at the start of each academic semester. If you pay your fees directly to the University, you can meanwhile choose to pay in instalments.

Additional course costs

There may be extra costs related to your course for things such as stationery, books, materials, as well as field trips.

Teaching

Program

The University of Chenab’s Department of Business Education provides an undergraduate program, Bachelor of Studies in Accounting and Finance (BSAF). It is for students with a particular interest in the rapidly growing field of business. It is a four-year, full-time course. Hence, it equips students with professional skills and knowledge required for contemporary national and international businesses. It helps students to learn about financial institutions, and various organizations. The BSAF program combines the study of Accounting with Finance. This helps to develop a broader business perspective and in-depth knowledge of fundamental concepts of financial management, corporate accounting, investment analysis, capital budgeting, as well as economic processing in global financial markets.

Awareness

The program places a strong emphasis on critical awareness of accounting and finance practices, providing students with the necessary skills to maintain a financial report on an organization’s performance and describe its financial structure as well as viability. Furthermore, students learn how to evaluate the role of an organization in raising money and capital, as well as allocate resources among competing investment and savings opportunities through Finance.

Case studies

Real-life case studies and work experience help develop students’ leadership skills, communication, and teamwork abilities. Through a wide range of courses, students have the option to focus on accounting, finance, or a combination of both. An optional placement year provides students with the opportunity to gain work experience with renowned national as well as international employers, including accountancy firms and investment banks. Alternatively, students may choose to pursue coursework instead of a placement year. Overall, the BSAF program is hence designed to cultivate skilled and proficient individuals in the fields of accounting and finance.

All the modules are assessed by coursework, including individual assignments, case study analysis, projects, online quizzes, group work, and presentations.

Careers

Benefits

After completing a Bachelor of Studies in Accounting and Finance, graduates can pursue various career opportunities in Pakistan and abroad. Here are a few examples:

Accountant:

Graduates can work as accountants for public accounting firms, private companies, as well as government organizations. They can perform tasks such as preparing financial statements, managing budgets, and hence ensuring compliance with tax laws and regulations.

Financial Analyst:

Graduates can work as financial analysts for investment banks, brokerage firms, as well as other financial institutions. They can analyze financial data, prepare financial models, and hence provide investment recommendations to clients.

Auditor:

Graduates can work as auditors for public accounting firms as well as government agencies. They can review financial records and systems, identify risks, as well as recommend improvements to internal controls.

Tax Consultant:

Graduates can work as tax consultants for public accounting firms or private companies. They can provide advice on tax planning, prepare tax returns, as well as assist with tax audits.

Financial Manager:

Graduates can work as financial managers for corporations or government agencies. They can oversee financial operations, manage budgets, as well as make strategic financial decisions.

Entrepreneur:

Graduates can hence start their own businesses, leveraging their skills in accounting and finance to manage their company’s finances.

In Pakistan, graduates can find job opportunities in public accounting firms, banks, financial institutions, and multinational corporations. Abroad, graduates can work in various industries, including finance, banking, accounting, as well as consulting firms. They can meanwhile pursue further education and certification, such as becoming a Chartered Accountant or a Certified Public Accountant, to enhance their career prospects.
