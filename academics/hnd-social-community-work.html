Program Overview

(HND) Social & Community Work

The Higher National Diploma (HND) in Social & Community Work at the University of Chenab is designed to provide students with a deep understanding of social and community work practices. This program prepares graduates to make a meaningful impact in various community and social settings by equipping them with the necessary skills and knowledge to address social issues and support individuals in need.

  • Internationally Recognized Qualification: Offered by Pearson Edexcel, London, UK.
  • Global Reach: Pearson Edexcel operates in 112 countries, with over four million students annually pursuing its internationally recognized qualifications.
  • Equivalent to Two-Year BA/BSc in Pakistan: Providing a solid foundation for further academic pursuits.
  • Affordable and Economical: Accessible education without compromising on quality.
  • Direct Employment Opportunities: In-depth professional education highly regarded by employers.
  • Pathway to Further Education: Successful completion of HND allows direct entry to a one-year top-up to a BA/BSc Honors Degree at UK universities.
  • Early Professional Training: Ideal for students looking to start their professional journey early.
  • Assignment-Based Assessment: UCI supports students in completing their HND on time.

The Pearson BTEC Level 5 Higher National Diploma is recognised by Higher Education providers as meeting admission requirements to many relevant social and community work-related courses, for example:

  • BA/BSc (Hons) in Social Work
  • BA (Hons) in Social and Community Work
  • BA (Hons) in Youth Work
  • BA (Hons) in Community Development
  • BA (Hons) in Working with Children, Young People and Families
  • BA (Hons) in Social Policy
  • BA (Hons) in Psychology
  • BA (Hons) in International Development
  • BA/BSc (Hons) in Health and Social Care.

Modules

Year 1: Core Foundations

  1. Introduction to Social and Community Work

    • Overview of social work principles, community engagement, and the role of social services.

  2. Human Development and Behaviour

    • Study of human growth, psychological development, and behavior patterns throughout the lifespan.

  3. Understanding Social Policy

    • Examination of social policies, their impact on communities, and how they shape social work practices.

  4. Communication and Interpersonal Skills

    • Development of effective communication techniques and interpersonal skills essential for social and community work.

  5. Professional Practice and Ethics

    • Exploration of ethical issues, professional standards, and reflective practice in social work.

  6. Community Needs Assessment

    • Methods and tools for assessing community needs and planning interventions.

Year 2: Advanced Topics and Specialization

  1. Community Development and Empowerment
    • Strategies for community development, empowerment, and promoting social change.

  2. Crisis Intervention and Support

    • Techniques for managing and supporting individuals in crisis situations.

  3. Group Work and Facilitation Skills

    • Skills for leading and facilitating group activities and support groups in community settings.

  4. Research Methods in Social Work

    • Introduction to research methods, data collection, and analysis relevant to social and community work.

  5. Health and Well-being in the Community

    • Focus on health promotion, prevention strategies, and improving community well-being.

  6. Integrated Care and Multi-agency Collaboration

    • Working with other agencies and professionals to provide integrated care and support services.

  7. Project Management in Social Work

    • Skills for managing social work projects, including planning, implementation, and evaluation.

  8. Work Placement and Practical Experience

    • Hands-on experience in a social or community work setting, applying theoretical knowledge in real-world scenarios.

Assessment

All the modules are assessed by coursework, including individual assignments, real-business case studies, interview projects, online quizzes, group work, and poster presentations.

Students are assessed by the follow methods:

Duration

Entry Requirement

At least 2 ‘A’ levels and 5 subjects of ‘O’ levels, Intermediate, Matric and Work-related experience. Appropriate English language skills.
