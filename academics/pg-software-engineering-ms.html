Software Engineering (MS)

Master of Science in Software engineering at the University of Chenab is an advanced-level graduate research degree program for technically minded students who aim to conduct research-related activities in software engineering fundamentals.

Program Overview

Master of Science in Software engineering at the University of Chenab is an advanced-level graduate research degree program for technically minded students who aim to conduct research-related activities in software engineering fundamentals.

Our Master’s degree is a full-time, two years degree program that provides a comprehensive knowledge of coursework as well as a research component. This advanced-level graduate research program emphasizes a rigorous foundation in technical tools and techniques necessary for providing creative solutions to complex computing problems. Through this course, our graduates will engage in active learning by balancing theoretical and applied knowledge of SE.

Our MS in Software Engineering will equip you with the expertise to develop software products and services through software engineering methods. The main objective is to develop fundamental knowledge, skills, and first-hand experience in software project management, reliability engineering, software testing, software measurements, and software risk management.

While studying software engineering, the opportunities for graduates to start their professional careers are extensive. There is a huge demand for software engineers in small and large corporations, industries, and in the fields of banking, education, and government. With specialization, you will have an option to pursue a career in research or continued studies towards a doctoral degree. It aims at strengthening the understanding of students for constructing computer programs including key technologies and skills needed for strong theoretical foundations and the practical ability to develop systems and products. This study will help you to understand and grasp a deep knowledge related to software assurance, Machine learning, cognitive neural networks, web-based system and information system development.

Eligibility Criteria

Before applying for a degree, you should first check that we recognize your undergraduate education qualification. This can include:

  1. Level 6 – Bachelor (Hons) [8-10 semesters with 124-140+ Credits Hours] in Software Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer System Engineering, Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Mathematics and Statistical Sciences, Data Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Software Project Management or any relevant subject.
  2. Level 6 – MA/MSc (16 years) in relevant subject described above.
  3. The following core courses are recommended to be completed before entering the program. 1. Human Computer Interaction 2. Software Construction & Development 3. Software Design & Architecture 4. Software Project Management 5. Software Quality Engineering 6. Software Re-Engineering 7. Software Requirements Engineering 8. Web Engineering 
  4. Minimum 2.0/4.0 CGPA in semester system or 60% in annual system examination are required. 

Tuition Fee

Tuition fees is the majority of the cost of you degree program. The University of Chenab charges the fess in the beginning of the academic year. You can also pay your fees in installments to the university.

Additional course costs
Some extra cost is charged because of the additional services such as books, stationery and other materials provided.

Funding
We award numerous postgraduate scholarships to students. Moreover, some external organizations also support the funding programs which in terms increase the net amount of scholarships.

Teaching

We use various methods of teachings through classroom activities including a combination of lectures, tutorials, lab sessions, seminars, workshops, online learning, and independent study. Program structure is comprised of a total number of 15-16 lectures and about 3 hours per week will be allocated for teaching that includes 58% practical and 42% theory. You will be guided all about the studies, mainly about time for preparing classes, completion of research projects, and reading of primary texts. Moreover, mode of study is English.

Assessment

Mainly evaluation will be concluded by extensive monitoring of written examination and course work completion along with routine quizzes, individual assignments, and group tasks such as writing, research and presentation.
