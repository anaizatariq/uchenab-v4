Admission at a glance

Choose your programme

Confirm the programme, duration, and eligibility criteria before applying.

Register online

Create your account on the UChenab online admission portal using a valid email and mobile number.

Complete the form

Enter your academic record, upload documents and select your programme preferences.

Pay the processing fee

Generate the challan from the portal and deposit it at the designated bank branch.

Entry test / interview

Appear for the departmental test or interview where the programme requires it.

Merit list & enrolment

Check the merit list, deposit the semester dues and complete your enrolment.

Key requirements

  • Attested copies of all academic transcripts, certificates and detailed marks certificates.
  • Attested copy of CNIC / B-Form of the applicant and CNIC of the father or guardian.
  • Recent passport-size photographs with a plain background.
  • Domicile certificate.
  • Migration certificate and character certificate from the last institution attended.
  • Equivalence certificate from IBCC for applicants with foreign qualifications.
